Start here从这里开始

No particle physics needed: civil engineers and geologists are welcome.不需要粒子物理基础：欢迎土木工程和地质学的同学。

Cosmic-ray muons rain down at about one per square centimetre per minute and cross hundreds of metres of rock. Counting how many get through a hillside, a dam or the ground above a tunnel reveals how much mass lies along each direction. This is muography, and Geant4 is the standard tool for simulating it before a detector goes into the field.

宇宙线缪子以每平方厘米每分钟约一个的速率落下，能穿过几百米的岩石。统计有多少缪子穿过了山坡、大坝或隧道上方的地层，就能知道每个方向上有多少质量。这就是缪子成像。在探测器进入现场之前，Geant4 是模拟它的标准工具。

How each week works每周如何学习

Follow the detector, not the class list.跟着探测器走，而不是跟着类列表走。

  1. 15 minRead the physics question and write a prediction读物理问题，写下预测
  2. 20 minRun the supplied example without changing code不改代码，先运行给定示例
  3. 30 minStudy the two or three lines that matter细读最关键的两三行代码
  4. 30 minChange one physical parameter and make a plot改变一个物理参数并作图
  5. 15 minCompare the result with your prediction把结果与预测比较
  6. 10 minWrite one limitation and one next question写下一个局限和下一个问题

If you cannot explain the plot without opening the source code, repeat the experiment before moving on.

如果不打开源代码就讲不清这张图，那就再做一遍实验，然后再往下走。

FAQ

Frequently asked questions常见问题

Do I need a particle-physics background?需要粒子物理基础吗？

No. Read the background primer first (about 45 minutes): it explains muons, cosmic rays, the units and muography in plain language. Every week also opens with a background section. You do need to be comfortable with a terminal; C++ experience helps but Week 1 starts in Python.

不需要。先读入门背景（约 45 分钟），它用通俗的语言讲解缪子、宇宙线、相关单位和缪子成像。每一周开头也有一节背景知识。你需要会使用终端；有 C++ 经验更好，但第 1 周从 Python 开始。

What software do I need?需要什么软件？

Week 1 needs only Python 3 with NumPy. Weeks 2–8, 10 and 11 need Geant4 11.4 (C++17, CMake 3.16 or newer) and Python with numpy, scipy, matplotlib, uproot and awkward. Week 9 and the musrSim parts of Week 10 use Geant4 10.7 with ROOT, kept in a separate environment.

第 1 周只需要 Python 3 和 NumPy。第 2–8、10、11 周需要 Geant4 11.4（C++17、CMake 3.16 或更新）以及装有 numpy、scipy、matplotlib、uproot、awkward 的 Python。第 9 周和第 10 周的 musrSim 部分使用 Geant4 10.7 与 ROOT，请放在单独的环境里。

How much time does it take?需要多少时间？

About 2 to 3 hours a week, about 30 hours in all. Every item on a week page shows its time estimate; the lab takes most of it.

每周约 2–3 小时，共约 30 小时。周页面上每一项都标有预计时间，实验占大部分。

Which language is the course in?课程用什么语言？

Every page is written in both English and Chinese. Use the language switch at the top: EN, Chinese, or both side by side; the combined view, which helps if you are learning the technical terms in a second language.

每一页都同时有英文和中文。用页面顶部的 EN · 中文 · EN+中 切换；EN+中 会同时显示两种语言，适合想同时掌握两种语言术语的同学。

Where is my progress saved?我的进度保存在哪里？

Only in this browser, on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere. Clearing the site data, or opening the course in a private window, starts from zero.

只保存在当前设备的这个浏览器里，不会发送到任何地方。清除网站数据或在无痕窗口中打开，进度会从零开始。

Are the numbers on the pages real?页面上的数字是真实的吗？

Yes. Expected outputs, histograms and three of the event displays come from our own Geant4 11.4.p01 runs. Anything simplified is labelled TOY MODEL and lists its approximations.

是的。预期输出、直方图和其中三个事例显示来自我们自己的 Geant4 11.4.p01 运行。凡是简化的内容都标有 TOY MODEL（玩具模型）并列出近似。
