Build a world, then watch the invisible, one step at a time.搭建世界，再一步一步看见不可见之物。

Geometry, material and placement are three separate decisions. You build a 10 cm block, swap its material in one line, and print every step a muon takes through it. A crossing that looks like one straight line turns out to be a short list of steps, each ended by a boundary or a physics process.

几何、材料和放置是三个相互独立的决定。你将搭建一个 10 cm 的方块，只改一行就换掉它的材料，并打印缪子穿过它的每一步。看起来是一条直线的穿越，其实是一串短短的步，每一步结束于边界或某个物理过程。

/control/echo backgroundbackground · for non-physicists背景知识 · 写给非物理专业

A muon does not see shapes, it sees grams per square centimetre.缪子看不见形状，它“看见”的是每平方厘米多少克。

Background · Shape, material, placement形状、材料、放置

Geant4 separates three decisions an engineer makes anyway: the geometry of a part (solid), what it is made of (logical volume with a material), and where it sits (physical placement). A tunnel lining, a rock layer and a detector plane are each built the same way.

Geant4 把工程师本来就要做的三个决定分开：部件的几何（实体）、它由什么做成（带材料的逻辑体）、它放在哪里（物理放置）。隧道衬砌、岩层和探测平面都是这样搭建的。

Background · Materials for engineers给工程师的材料说明

Geant4 ships a NIST database: G4_CONCRETE (2.3 g/cm³), G4_WATER, G4_Fe, G4_Pb, G4_SILICON_DIOXIDE and many more. Rock is usually defined by hand from its measured density and its main oxides or elements. For muon absorption the density matters most; the composition shifts the energy loss by a few percent.

Geant4 自带 NIST 材料库：G4_CONCRETE（2.3 g/cm³）、G4_WATER、G4_Fe、G4_Pb、G4_SILICON_DIOXIDE 等。岩石通常要根据实测密度和主要氧化物或元素手工定义。对缪子吸收来说，密度最重要；成分只会让能量损失改变几个百分点。

Background · Areal density: the number that matters面密度：真正重要的数

Density × thickness, in g/cm². Ten metres of concrete (2.3 g/cm³) is 2 300 g/cm², the same as 23 m of water or about 8.7 m of standard rock (2.65 g/cm³). Muography measures this quantity along each direction, not density or thickness alone.

密度 × 厚度，单位 g/cm²。10 米混凝土（2.3 g/cm³）是 2 300 g/cm²，等于 23 米水，或约 8.7 米标准岩石（2.65 g/cm³）。缪子成像测量的是沿每个方向的这个量，而不是单独的密度或厚度。

For muography projects用于缪子成像工程

A site model is this week's skill at scale: terrain from a digital elevation model, layers from boreholes, a tunnel from the design drawings, often imported through CAD/GDML. Run the overlap check after every change; an overlap between rock layers silently doubles or loses mass along some lines of sight.

场地模型就是本周技能的放大版：地形来自数字高程模型，地层来自钻孔，隧道来自设计图纸，常通过 CAD/GDML 导入。每次修改后都要做重叠检查；岩层之间的重叠会在某些视线上悄悄多算或漏算质量。

/control/manual3.1 · three decisions3.1 · 三个决定

What shape, what material, and where: three questions, three classes.什么形状、什么材料、放在哪里：三个问题，三个类。

Definition 3.1 · The three-layer geometry abstraction三层几何抽象

G4VSolid describes pure geometric shape and carries no material or position. G4LogicalVolume binds a solid to a material (and later to a sensitive detector, a field and other properties). G4PVPlacement places a logical volume at a position and orientation inside a mother volume. The same logical volume can be placed many times without redefining its shape or material.

G4VSolid 只描述几何形状，不带材料和位置信息；G4LogicalVolume 把实体与材料绑定（之后还会绑定灵敏探测器、磁场等）；G4PVPlacement 把逻辑体以一定位置和取向放进母体。同一个逻辑体可以多次放置，无需重新定义形状或材料。

G4VSolid → G4LogicalVolume → G4PVPlacement
pattern (shape)    fabric (material)    where it hangs (placement)

Intuition直觉

Think of pattern, fabric and placement. The pattern (solid) decides shirt or coat; the fabric (material on the logical volume) decides cotton or silk; the same garment can hang in different places in a closet (placement). Moving it does not re-cut the pattern, and changing the fabric does not redesign it.

把这三层想成纸样、布料和挂放位置：纸样（实体）决定是衬衫还是外套；布料（逻辑体上的材料）决定是棉还是丝；同一件衣服可以挂在衣柜的不同位置（放置）。移动它不必重新裁剪，换布料也不必重新设计纸样。

Example 3.1 · A 10 × 10 × 10 cm³ block of concrete10 × 10 × 10 cm³ 混凝土块

用 G4Box 定义半边长 5 cm 的立方体；用 G4NistManager::FindOrBuildMaterial("G4_CONCRETE") 获取混凝土；用 G4PVPlacement 放在世界中心。然后依次换成 G4_Al、G4_Cu、G4_Pb。形状和放置代码始终不变，只改一个材料字符串。

When the detector comes from CAD: GDML当探测器来自 CAD：GDML

A real detector often starts as a CAD model from the mechanical team. Geant4 reads geometry written in GDML (Geometry Description Markup Language) with G4GDMLParser: CAD → GDML export → parser.Read("detector.gdml") → parser.GetWorldVolume() → overlap check. A GDML file is not automatically a more accurate simulation. CAD assemblies carry screws, cosmetic surfaces and duplicate solids that cost tracking time and change nothing. Export a simplified model, check its dimensions and materials against the drawing, and add only geometry that can change an observable. GDML support needs a Geant4 build with Xerces-C; geant4-config --has-feature gdml tells you.

真实的探测器常常始于机械组的 CAD 模型。Geant4 通过 G4GDMLParser 读取 GDML（几何描述标记语言）：CAD → 导出 GDML → parser.Read("detector.gdml") → parser.GetWorldVolume() → 重叠检查。GDML 文件并不自动意味着更准确的模拟：CAD 装配体里有螺丝、装饰面和重复实体，只会拖慢径迹计算而不改变任何结果。先导出简化模型，对照图纸核对尺寸和材料，只加入可能改变可观测量的几何。GDML 需要带 Xerces-C 编译的 Geant4，可用 geant4-config --has-feature gdml 查看。

/tracking/storeTrajectory 13.2 · one crossing, many steps3.2 · 一次穿越，许多步

A step is the free flight between two interruptions.一步，就是两次“打断”之间的自由飞行。

Truth versus measurement真相与测量

A G4Step is simulation truth: what happened during one transport step. A real detector never sees a step. It sees deposited energy, scintillation light, charge, or a threshold crossing. Week 5 builds that distinction into a detector.

G4Step 是模拟真相：一个输运步中发生了什么。真实探测器从来看不到“步”，它看到的是沉积能量、闪烁光、电荷或越过阈值。第 5 周会把这一区分落实到探测器中。

Definition 3.2 · Physics processes and steps物理过程与步

A physics process describes one mechanism by which a particle interacts with matter: ionisation, multiple Coulomb scattering, bremsstrahlung. Whenever a process occurs, or the particle reaches a geometric boundary, Geant4 ends the current step, records its start and end position, momentum, kinetic energy, deposited energy and the name of the process that ended it, and begins the next step. A track is the concatenation of its steps.

物理过程描述粒子与物质相互作用的一种机制：电离、多次库仑散射、轫致辐射。每当某个过程发生，或粒子到达几何边界，Geant4 就结束当前步，记录其起止位置、动量、动能、沉积能量以及结束它的过程名，然后开始下一步。径迹就是这些步的串联。

Example 3.2 · Colouring tracks by process按过程给径迹着色

In SteppingAction, read the name of the process that ended each step and colour that segment. For the 1 GeV muon the names are muIoni (ionisation with a δ-ray above threshold), Transportation (a boundary), and rarely muBrems or muPairProd. The δ-ray electrons it knocks out show eIoni, eBrem and msc: short, sharply bent tracks.

在 SteppingAction 中读取结束每一步的过程名，并据此给该段着色。对 1 GeV 缪子，名称是 muIoni（产生超过阈值 δ 电子的电离）、Transportation（边界），偶尔有 muBrems 或 muPairProd。被打出的 δ 电子显示 eIoni、eBrem 和 msc：短而急剧弯曲的径迹。

Code microscope · SteppingAction.ccGEANT4
G4StepPoint* postStepPoint = step->GetPostStepPoint();
const G4VProcess* process = postStepPoint->GetProcessDefinedStep();   // who ended THIS step
G4double edep = step->GetTotalEnergyDeposit();                          // not the kinetic energy

Two idioms recur in every later week. A step has a pre-step point and a post-step point; GetProcessDefinedStep() is read from the post-step point because that is where Geant4 records which process ended the step. And GetTotalEnergyDeposit(), not the track's kinetic energy, is what Week 4 accumulates. Keep "energy lost" and "energy deposited here" apart from the first day.

有两个惯用法会在之后每周反复出现。一步有前步点和后步点；GetProcessDefinedStep() 要从后步点读取，因为 Geant4 在那里记录是哪个过程结束了这一步。第 4 周要累加的是 GetTotalEnergyDeposit()，而不是径迹的动能。从第一天起就把“损失的能量”和“沉积在此处的能量”分开。

History历史

C. T. R. Wilson's cloud chamber (1911) made charged tracks visible as droplet trails. In 1936 Anderson and Neddermeyer used one to find, in cosmic rays, a particle whose curvature fell between the electron and the proton: the muon. A stepping action does the cloud chamber's job in software.

威尔逊（C. T. R. Wilson）的云室（1911 年）让带电粒子的径迹以雾滴轨迹的形式显现。1936 年，安德森和尼德迈耶用云室在宇宙线中发现了一种曲率介于电子和质子之间的粒子——缪子。步进动作就是用软件来做云室的工作。

A second viewer: VRML and ParaView第二种可视化：VRML 与 ParaView

Qt/OpenGL is good for watching one event while you develop. The VRML2FILE driver writes the scene to a .wrl file (named like g4_00.wrl) that you can open later, on another machine, in ParaView's VRML 2.0 reader. ParaView reads the geometry as polygons; it is not an event display and drops Geant4's event metadata. Record one difference that is purely a viewer effect and one feature that reveals a real geometry problem.

Qt/OpenGL 适合在开发时观察单个事件。VRML2FILE 驱动把场景写入 .wrl 文件（如 g4_00.wrl），之后可以在另一台机器上用 ParaView 的 VRML 2.0 读取器打开。ParaView 把几何读成多边形，它不是事件显示器，也不保留 Geant4 的事件元数据。请记下一处纯属显示效果的差异，和一处暴露真实几何问题的特征。

    nullptr, G4ThreeVector(), logicBlock, "Block",
    logicWorld, false, 0, true);  // last argument: check overlaps at placement

  return physWorld;
}

    src/SteppingAction.ccGEANT4
    #include "SteppingAction.hh"

#include "G4Step.hh"
#include "G4SystemOfUnits.hh"
#include "G4VProcess.hh"
#include "G4ios.hh"

void SteppingAction::UserSteppingAction(const G4Step* step)
{
  G4StepPoint* postStepPoint = step->GetPostStepPoint();

  // the process that ended THIS step (transportation counts too)
  const G4VProcess* process = postStepPoint->GetProcessDefinedStep();
  G4String processName = process ? process->GetProcessName() : "none";

  G4double edep = step->GetTotalEnergyDeposit();
  G4double stepLen = step->GetStepLength();
  G4ThreeVector pos = postStepPoint->GetPosition();

  // Two additions to the minimal listing, so the output can be filtered:
  // the volume the step happened in, and the track ID (1 = the primary).
  const G4String& volume = step->GetPreStepPoint()->GetPhysicalVolume()->GetName();
  G4int trackID = step->GetTrack()->GetTrackID();

  G4cout << " step ends via " << processName
         << " in " << volume << " trk " << trackID
         << " edep=" << edep / keV << " keV"
         << " stepLen=" << stepLen / mm << " mm"
         << " at " << pos / cm << " cm" << G4endl;
}

#include "G4Event.hh"
#include "G4ParticleGun.hh"
#include "G4ParticleTable.hh"
#include "G4SystemOfUnits.hh"

PrimaryGeneratorAction::PrimaryGeneratorAction()
{
  G4int n_particle = 1;
  fParticleGun = new G4ParticleGun(n_particle);

  // The four choices that define the experiment (as in Week 2):
  G4ParticleTable* particleTable = G4ParticleTable::GetParticleTable();
  G4ParticleDefinition* particle = particleTable->FindParticle("mu-");
  fParticleGun->SetParticleDefinition(particle);                          // 1. species
  fParticleGun->SetParticleMomentumDirection(G4ThreeVector(0., 0., 1.));  // 2. direction
  fParticleGun->SetParticleEnergy(1. * GeV);                              // 3. kinetic energy
  fParticleGun->SetParticlePosition(G4ThreeVector(0., 0., -0.4 * m));     // 4. position: inside the 1 m world
}

PrimaryGeneratorAction::~PrimaryGeneratorAction()
{
  delete fParticleGun;
}

void PrimaryGeneratorAction::GeneratePrimaries(G4Event* event)
{
  // Called once per Event; this is where the primary vertex
  // and the primary particle's Track are actually created.
  fParticleGun->GeneratePrimaryVertex(event);
}

    steps_summary.pyANALYSIS
    """Summarise the SteppingAction printout of week03: per event, count the primary
muon's steps inside the Block (by process) and sum the energy deposited in the Block.

Usage: ./week03 run100.mac > concrete.log && python3 steps_summary.py concrete.log
"""
import collections
import math
import re
import sys

PAT = re.compile(r" step ends via (\S+) in (\S+) trk (\d+) edep=(\S+) keV "
                 r"stepLen=(\S+) mm at \((\S+),(\S+),(\S+)\) cm")


def events(lines):
    """A new event starts when the primary (trk 1) reappears after other tracks,
    or when its z coordinate jumps back (an event with no secondaries)."""
    cur, prev_trk, last_z = None, None, None
    for line in lines:
        m = PAT.match(line)
        if not m:
            continue
        proc, vol, trk, edep, _, _, _, z = m.groups()
        trk, edep, z = int(trk), float(edep), float(z)
        if trk == 1 and (cur is None or prev_trk != 1 or z < last_z - 1e-6):
            if cur is not None:
                yield cur
            cur = {"steps": collections.Counter(), "edep_MeV": 0.0}
        if trk == 1:
            last_z = z
        prev_trk = trk
        if vol == "Block":
            cur["edep_MeV"] += edep / 1000.0
            if trk == 1:
                cur["steps"][proc] += 1
    if cur is not None:
        yield cur


def main(path):
    evs = list(events(open(path)))
    n = len(evs)
    steps = [sum(e["steps"].values()) for e in evs]
    edep = [e["edep_MeV"] for e in evs]
    procs = collections.Counter()
    for e in evs:
        procs.update(e["steps"])

    def mean_err(a):
        m = sum(a) / len(a)
        sd = math.sqrt(sum((v - m) ** 2 for v in a) / len(a))
        return m, sd / math.sqrt(len(a))

    m_s, e_s = mean_err(steps)
    m_e, e_e = mean_err(edep)
    print(f"events                  {n}")
    print(f"primary steps in Block  {m_s:.2f} +/- {e_s:.2f}")
    for proc, c in procs.most_common():
        print(f"   {proc:<20s} {c / n:.2f} per event")
    print(f"Edep in Block [MeV]     {m_e:.2f} +/- {e_e:.2f}")


if __name__ == "__main__":
    main(sys.argv[1])

    Think about it, answered: lead against liquid hydrogen“想一想”的答案：铅与液氢
    
          
    The second Think-about-it asked whether switching lead for liquid hydrogen (160× less dense) gives more steps or fewer. The scan answers it: 2.21 against 7.67 per crossing. muIoni steps are δ-rays above threshold, and hydrogen has far fewer electrons per cm. The lower threshold in lH2 (88 keV against 1.01 MeV) claws back part of the difference, which is why the ratio is about 3.5, not 160. Check this with the toy model above.
    
          
    第二个“想一想”问：把铅换成液氢（密度低约 160 倍），步数会更多还是更少？扫描给出答案：每次穿越 2.21 步对 7.67 步。muIoni 步就是超过阈值的 δ 电子，而氢每厘米的电子少得多。液氢的阈值更低（88 keV，铅为 1.01 MeV），挽回了一部分差距，所以比值约为 3.5 而不是 160。可以在上面的玩具模型中验证。
