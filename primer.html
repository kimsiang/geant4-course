The physics you need before Week 1, if you come from civil engineering or geology.如果你来自土木工程或地质学，这是第 1 周之前你需要的物理。

You do not need a particle-physics degree to simulate muography. You need about a dozen ideas: what a muon is, where it comes from, how much rock it can cross, what it does when it crosses, and how a detector notices. This page explains each in engineering language, with the numbers you will use, and shows where every week of the course fits into a real muography project.

/particle/select mu-1 · what a muon is1 · 缪子是什么

A heavy, short-lived cousin of the electron.电子的一个又重又短命的“表亲”。

Ordinary matter is made of atoms: a small, heavy nucleus surrounded by light electrons. The muon is an elementary particle with the same electric charge as the electron (μ⁻) or its opposite (μ⁺), but about 207 times heavier. It is not part of ordinary atoms, because it is unstable: on average it lives 2.197 microseconds and then decays into an electron (or positron) and two neutrinos, which escape unseen.

普通物质由原子构成：一个又小又重的原子核，外面围着轻的电子。缪子是一种基本粒子，电荷与电子相同（μ⁻）或相反（μ⁺），但质量约为电子的 207 倍。它不是普通原子的组成部分，因为它不稳定：平均寿命 2.197 微秒，之后衰变为一个电子（或正电子）和两个中微子，中微子会悄无声息地逃走。

Two properties make the muon useful to engineers. Because it is heavy, it is not easily deflected or stopped: it ploughs through hundreds of metres of rock in nearly a straight line. Because it is charged, it leaves a trail of ionised atoms that a detector can record. A muon is therefore a natural, free, and penetrating probe, rather like an X-ray that can see through a mountain.

有两个性质让缪子对工程师有用。因为重，它不容易被偏转或挡住：能以近乎直线的路径穿过几百米岩石。因为带电，它沿途会留下一串电离的原子，探测器可以记录下来。所以缪子是一种天然、免费、穿透力极强的探针，有点像能穿透一座山的 X 射线。

Energy units without fear别怕能量单位

Particle physicists measure energy in electron-volts: 1 eV is the energy an electron gains across 1 volt, 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ J. Then 1 MeV = 10⁶ eV and 1 GeV = 10⁹ eV. Mass and momentum are quoted in the same units (MeV/c², MeV/c), so you can add and compare them directly. Rule of thumb: a cosmic muon of a few GeV carries the energy of a few ten-billionths of a joule. It is tiny for a person and enormous for a single atom.

粒子物理用电子伏特计量能量：1 eV 是一个电子经过 1 伏电压获得的能量，即 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ J。1 MeV = 10⁶ eV，1 GeV = 10⁹ eV。质量和动量也用同样的单位表示（MeV/c²、MeV/c），可以直接相加和比较。经验法则：几个 GeV 的宇宙线缪子所带能量只有几十亿分之一焦耳，对人来说微不足道，对单个原子来说却巨大无比。

/gun/particle mu-2 · where cosmic muons come from2 · 宇宙线缪子从哪里来

A steady rain from 15 km up, about one per square centimetre per minute.从 15 公里高空落下的稳定“雨”，每平方厘米每分钟约一个。

The Earth is constantly hit by cosmic rays, mostly protons from outside the solar system. When one strikes a nucleus of the upper atmosphere it produces a shower of new particles, among them pions, which quickly decay into muons. Most muons are produced high in the atmosphere, typically around 15 km.

地球一直受到宇宙线的轰击，主要是来自太阳系外的质子。它撞上高层大气的原子核时，会产生一簇新粒子，其中有 π 介子，π 介子很快衰变成缪子。大多数缪子产生于大气高层，典型高度约 15 公里。

Why muons reach the ground at all缪子为什么能到达地面

In 2.2 µs light travels only 660 m, so a muon "should" decay long before falling 15 km. It doesn't, because it moves at nearly the speed of light and special relativity slows its clock. A muon with 4 GeV of energy has a time-dilation factor γ ≈ 38, so its average decay length grows to about 25 km. Cosmic muons are an everyday proof of relativity.

2.2 µs 内光只能走 660 m，所以缪子“本该”在落下 15 公里之前就衰变掉。但它没有，因为它以接近光速运动，狭义相对论让它的时钟变慢。能量 4 GeV 的缪子时间膨胀因子 γ ≈ 38，平均衰变长度因此增加到约 25 公里。宇宙线缪子是相对论的日常证据。

"sr" is the steradian, the unit of solid angle: how big a patch of sky a detector looks at. A full sky hemisphere is 2π ≈ 6.28 sr. Rates in muography always come as intensity × area × solid angle × time.

“sr”是球面度，立体角的单位：表示探测器看到的那片天空有多大。整个半边天空是 2π ≈ 6.28 sr。缪子成像中的计数率总是“强度 × 面积 × 立体角 × 时间”。

/run/beamOn3 · muons in rock3 · 缪子在岩石中

What matters is mass per area, not thickness.关键是单位面积的质量，而不是厚度。

A muon crossing matter loses energy mainly by knocking electrons off atoms along its path (ionisation, Week 4). The loss is steady, like friction, and depends almost only on how much mass it crosses. Physicists therefore measure thickness as areal density, density × length, in g/cm². One metre of standard rock (2.65 g/cm³) is 265 g/cm². The same quantity expressed in metres of water is the metre water equivalent (m.w.e.): 1 m.w.e. = 100 g/cm².

缪子穿过物质时，主要靠沿途把原子的电子打出来而损失能量（电离，第 4 周）。这种损失像摩擦一样稳定，几乎只取决于穿过了多少质量。所以物理学家用面密度（密度 × 长度，单位 g/cm²）来衡量厚度。1 米标准岩石（2.65 g/cm³）就是 265 g/cm²。同一个量用水的米数表示，就是米水当量（m.w.e.）：1 m.w.e. = 100 g/cm²。

In rock a muon loses roughly 2 MeV per g/cm², about 0.5 GeV per metre of rock. Above a few hundred GeV, radiative losses (bremsstrahlung, pair production) add a term that grows with energy; in standard rock the two are equal near 500 GeV. Read the table backwards and it becomes the core of absorption muography: under 19 m of rock only muons that arrived with more than about 10 GeV survive. Because the sky's energy spectrum falls steeply, every extra metre of rock removes a predictable fraction of muons. Count fewer muons than expected along one direction, and there is more mass along that line; count more, and there is a void.

把这张表倒过来读，就是吸收式缪子成像的核心：在 19 m 岩石下，只有到达时能量高于约 10 GeV 的缪子能活下来。天空的能谱下降很陡，所以每多一米岩石，就会按可预测的比例去掉一部分缪子。某个方向上测到的缪子比预期少，说明那条线上质量更多；测到的更多，说明有空洞。

Scattering: the second effect散射：第二种效应

Each muon also zig-zags slightly as it passes many nuclei (multiple Coulomb scattering, Week 4). The typical angle grows with the square root of thickness, shrinks with momentum, and is larger in heavy elements such as iron or lead. A 3 GeV muon crossing 1 m of standard rock is deflected by about 0.9° (PDG formula with radiation length 26.5 g/cm²). This blurs absorption images, but it is also a signal in its own right: scattering muography detects dense, high-Z objects such as steel or uranium inside containers.

缪子经过大量原子核时还会轻微地“之”字形偏折（多次库仑散射，第 4 周）。典型偏转角随厚度的平方根增大，随动量增大而减小，在铁、铅这类重元素中更大。3 GeV 的缪子穿过 1 m 标准岩石，偏转约 0.9°（按 PDG 公式，辐射长度取 26.5 g/cm²）。这会让吸收成像变模糊，但它本身也是一种信号：散射式缪子成像能探测集装箱里的钢材或铀这类高密度、高原子序数物体。

/vis/drawVolume4 · muography4 · 缪子成像

Muography began as a civil-engineering measurement.缪子成像最早就是一次土木工程测量。

In 1955 E. P. George used cosmic-ray muons to measure the rock overburden above a tunnel of the Snowy Mountains hydro-electric scheme in Australia. In the late 1960s Luis Alvarez searched the Chephren pyramid for hidden chambers. In 2017 the ScanPyramids team reported a large previously unknown void in Khufu's pyramid (Nature 552, 386). Today muography is used on volcanoes, mines, tunnels, dams, historical buildings and nuclear reactors.

1955 年，E. P. George 用宇宙线缪子测量了澳大利亚雪山水电工程一条隧道上方的岩石覆盖厚度。1960 年代末，Luis Alvarez 在哈夫拉金字塔中寻找隐藏的墓室。2017 年，ScanPyramids 团队报告在胡夫金字塔中发现一个此前未知的大空腔（Nature 552, 386）。如今缪子成像被用于火山、矿山、隧道、大坝、历史建筑和核反应堆。

The price of a free probe is time. Muons cannot be switched on, focused or made brighter, so a survey collects them for days to months, and the question is always: how long until the feature I am looking for stands out from statistical noise? That is exactly the question a simulation answers before anyone carries a detector into a tunnel.

免费探针的代价是时间。缪子不能打开、聚焦或调亮，所以一次勘测要收集几天到几个月，而问题永远是：要多久，我要找的特征才能从统计涨落中显现出来？这正是在任何人把探测器搬进隧道之前，模拟要回答的问题。

/control/manual5 · what Geant4 does for a muography project5 · Geant4 为缪子成像项目做什么

A virtual site survey you can run before the real one.一次可以先于真实勘测进行的虚拟勘测。

Geant4 is a free C++ toolkit, developed at CERN and used from the LHC to radiotherapy and spacecraft design, that moves particles through a geometry you describe and applies the physics of how they interact. For a muography project it plays the role that a finite-element model plays in structural engineering: you build the model, run the load case (here, a sky full of muons), and read off the response. The course teaches each link of this chain:

Geant4 是一个免费的 C++ 工具包，由 CERN 开发，应用范围从大型强子对撞机到放射治疗和航天器设计。它让粒子在你描述的几何中运动，并施加它们相互作用的物理规律。对缪子成像项目来说，它的角色相当于结构工程中的有限元模型：建好模型，施加工况（这里是满天的缪子），读出响应。课程教的就是这条链上的每一环：

The computing you need你需要的计算技能

A Linux or macOS terminal (cd, ls, editing a text file); enough C++ to change a number in a class and recompile (the course shows you which line); Python with NumPy for analysis. If C++ is new to you, spend an evening on a basic tutorial covering variables, functions and classes before Week 2. You do not need to write a Geant4 application from scratch: every week starts from working code.

会用 Linux 或 macOS 终端（cd、ls、编辑文本文件）；掌握足以修改类中的一个数值并重新编译的 C++（课程会告诉你改哪一行）；会用 Python 和 NumPy 做分析。如果你没接触过 C++，在第 2 周之前花一个晚上看一个入门教程，了解变量、函数和类即可。你不需要从零写 Geant4 程序：每周都从能运行的代码开始。
