# Simulated pilot, October 2026

**These are AI-simulated students, not real people.** Ten personas (civil engineer, volcanologist, physics undergraduate, HEP PhD student, medical physicist, part-time NDT engineer, μSR postdoc, C++ engineer, low-vision phone user, sceptical industry buyer) worked through the published pages and code, compiled and ran the labs against Geant4 11.4 where they could (dataset-free test physics list), and answered every item of `../questionnaire.md`.

Each persona has a `.json` (all answers + issues with page, section, severity, quote and fix) and a `.md` (readable review). The issues were verified against the files and fixed in the commit that adds this folder; see the pilot report for what changed and what is still open. Use the questionnaire with your real cohort to confirm or overturn these findings.
