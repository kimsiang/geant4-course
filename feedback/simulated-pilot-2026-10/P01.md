# P01 · 李明 (Li Ming) — pilot feedback, Geant4 2.0

**About me.** I am a second-year MSc student in tunnel engineering in Chengdu. My supervisor wants to know if muography can find voids above our metro tunnel. I am good at structural mechanics, ABAQUS and MATLAB, and I know a little Python (pandas). I have never written C++ and never used Linux. I have one Windows 11 laptop (14-inch). I study in 中文 mode and switch to EN only to check terms, and I have no VPN at home. I can give the course about 6 hours a week. I am on the muography track and read the primer first.

**What I read.** I read every section of the primer and Weeks 1, 2, 3, 4, 11 and 12, including the labs and code. I skimmed Weeks 5–8 (headings, labs and the muography notes), and for Weeks 9–10 I read only the muography notes.

## What works for me

- **The primer is the best part.** It speaks to an engineer: areal density instead of thickness, the figure showing 1 m of rock = 1.15 m of concrete = 2.65 m of water, the PDG standard-rock range table, and "Geant4 is like an FEM model: build the model, apply the load case, read the response." The project-step-to-week table told me exactly why each week matters.
- **Every week has a "用于缪子成像工程" note.** I read all of them. Week 8's note (field counts ÷ simulated counts, same reconstruction code) and Week 12's background question ("1 m² 望远镜，能否在一个月内探测到拱顶以上 20 m 范围内一个 3 m 的空洞？") describe my thesis in one sentence.
- **The Week 1 toy and Week 4's Bethe toy made ideas click.** I saw 1/√N with my own eyes, and I finally understood why the most probable value and the mean are different.
- **Week 11's debug tree and validation ladder are V&V language** I already know from computational mechanics.
- **The Chinese is mostly natural,** much better than machine translation. Numbers I could check (1 m rock = 265 g/cm², 10 GeV range ≈ 19 m, 0.9° scattering) are right.

## Where I get stuck

**1. I cannot install Geant4 from this course (blocker).** Week 2 says "需要 Geant4 11.4 和 CMake ≥ 3.16（第 1 周第 7 步）". But Week 1 step 7 only says "加载 Geant4 与 ROOT 环境后运行：geant4-config --version". No page, FAQ answer or README section says how to install it. Windows is never mentioned. The landing page says "container provided for Windows", but I cannot find that container anywhere. On my own I would ask DeepSeek, discover WSL2, then fight the 2 GB dataset download from cern.ch without a VPN. That is 4–8 hours. Self-paced, I would probably stop in Week 2.

**2. Week 1 step 1 is Linux-only.** `python3 -m venv ~/g4c && source ~/g4c/bin/activate` does not work in PowerShell. `pip install numpy` is very slow without the Tsinghua mirror. The step says nothing about mirrors for pip, conda, the Geant4 datasets or GitHub (Week 12's `git push --tags`). Week 3's `sed -i` loop and Week 12's `/usr/bin/time -v` are bash-only too.

**3. Pages are blank for 15 seconds in China.** The Google Fonts `<link>` blocks rendering. In my test, with the font request hanging, first paint came at 15.2–16.4 s on every page I tried. The 3D displays then show a black box for about 20 s before the GIF appears, and the GIF labels are English only.

**4. The course promises my tunnel-void project but never builds it.** The course home lists "结课项目：隧道上方空洞、覆盖层厚度或大坝密度", and Week 12 says "第 10 周的程序就是一个完整模板". But Week 10 (μSR) is optional for my track. No Geant4 lab ever uses standard rock, a tunnel or a void. The Week 5/8 generators shoot 4 GeV muons ("a toy choice") with no energy spectrum. Week 12's background says "用本页的可行性规划器代入你场地的数字", but the planner has no input for flux, area, overburden or days. Week 11 validates dE/dx only in 10 cm of plastic, not muon survival through metres of rock.

**5. There is no support.** Lab 11.1 is "教师搭建故障实验室". If I run `make_faults.sh` myself, its comments give away every fault. Week 12 needs "你的搭档" as reviewer. Week 1 and Week 2 ask me to post results to a "课程群" that is never linked.

## Smaller things

- **Terms are inconsistent.** "Event" is 事件 in Weeks 2–3 ("说出事件模型运行 → 事件 → 径迹 → 步") but 事例 in the glossary and everywhere else. "World" is sometimes left in English: "3 m 空 world", "飞出 world 的事例".
- **Some labels stay English in 中文 mode.** For example "Background ·", "Definition 1.1", "Code microscope" and the hero labels (Language / Observable / Deliverable).
- **Week 4 code and page disagree.** The `RunAction.cc` comment says "~28 MeV at 100 MeV", but the page box and the expected output say ~24 MeV (23.9456).
- **Week 3 points to a missing file.** The `steps_summary.py` docstring uses `run100.mac`, which does not exist.
- **Week 3 confuses me about the gun position.** It says "放在 −1 m 的粒子枪会在世界之外，见第 2 周", but in Week 2 the gun at −1 m was inside the world.
- **Week 7 has a misnumbered section.** One section is numbered "8.2 从理想磁场到实测场图".
- **ROOT is required too early.** Week 1 checks `root-config`, but my track never needs ROOT.
- **The environment script is never named.** The pages never say `geant4.sh`. No common-errors box covers the missing-dataset fatal error ("G4ENSDFSTATEDATA environment variable must be set").
- **There are no zip downloads.** I must create folders and paste about 10 files per lab (Week 5 is about 650 lines). For a non-programmer, one paste mistake means a lost evening.
