# P09 · 黄子轩 (Huang Zixuan): Geant4 2.0 on a phone, an iPad and a zoomed lab PC

**Who I am.** I'm a second-year applied physics student in Guangzhou. I have low vision, so I study with 200% browser zoom plus the OS magnifier, in dark mode. Most of my reading happens on an Android phone or an iPad during my commute. Labs happen on a shared lab PC where I have no admin rights. I use 中文 mode, I'm on the muography track, and I have about 5 hours a week. I read the primer and Weeks 1, 4, 7 and 10 in full. I skimmed Weeks 2, 3, 5, 6, 8, 9, 11 and 12.

**How I tested.** I used Playwright in dark mode with 中文 selected, at three sizes: phone (390×844), tablet (820×1180), and 1280×800 at 200% zoom (a 640×400 CSS viewport). The fonts and CDN hosts were unreachable, which is what a mainland connection looks like. I also ran a second pass with three.js served locally so I could see the real 3D. The screenshots are in `pilot/shots/P09-*.png`.

## What works well

- **Dark mode is really good.** axe-core reported no colour-contrast failures on any page I checked. No page scrolls sideways as a whole at any of my three sizes.
- **At 200% zoom the HTML reflows cleanly.** The item drawer (目录) is large and clear on the phone, with big rows, circles for progress and readable times.
- **The 3D displays respect touch.** "旋转关闭" is the default on touch screens, so swiping over a display scrolls the page. I checked this with real touch events on week07, week01, week04 and week10. Turning rotation on traps the swipe, as you would expect.
- **The toys work with my finger.** Pressing "/run/beamOn 10000" adds events. Week 10's play/pause, sliders and fit all work on the phone.
- **Week 1 matched on the lab PC.** My output matched the page digit for digit: `N=1000 eps=0.0570 … N=100000 eps=0.0482`.
- **The Chinese reads naturally.** It's clearly written by a person, not a machine, and the 用于缪子成像工程 boxes in every week are exactly what my track needs.

## What hurt me

1. **From China, the page depends on blocked hosts.** The `fonts.googleapis.com` stylesheet is render-blocking, and three.js comes from cdnjs and jsDelivr. When those hosts fail, every 3D display shows "此处无法显示 3D（库加载失败）". What I get instead is a 16:9 GIF inside a 4:5 phone box: big black bands, English-only labels, and text about 5 px tall ("rays entering the upper layer", "N 16 · crossed both 0"). For me that's unreadable. Without the web font, ε̂ in the Week 1 toy table shows as "εˆ= k/N".
2. **The SVG figures are cut off on the phone.** Below 640 px each figure gets `min-width: 620px` inside a sideways-scrolling box. Nothing tells you it scrolls, so the right ~40% just looks missing. In Week 10 that hides "f = (γμ/2π) B", "B 沿 +x，垂直纸面向里" and "U (+y)". At 200% zoom on the lab PC the column is 608 px and the figure is 620 px, so 12 px is clipped and a pointless scrollbar appears. This affects the primer (3 figures) and Weeks 1, 4, 7 and 10.
3. **The Week 7 toy has overlapping labels.** On the phone the top line of the canvas draws "俯视图：z →，x ↑（x 放大 ×30）" and "B = 1.50 T (y, out of page)" at the same y = 24, so neither can be read. The second label is also English-only.
4. **I couldn't install Geant4.** I have no admin rights on the shared lab PC, and the course has no conda-forge, Docker or cloud option. I was stuck from Week 2 on, so for Weeks 2–12 I only read the labs.
5. **Small targets and fixed small text.** Step checkboxes are 16×16. Range sliders are 16 px tall, which is very hard to grab with the magnifier. Other small targets: the "入门背景" link is 49×14, Week 10's "▶ play" is 65×22, and the brand link is 62×19. Canvas and HUD text is fixed at 10–11 px (`.evd-hud`, `.evd-labels`) and ignores my font settings.
6. **Expected-output tables need sideways scrolling on the phone.** In Week 7 step 3 and Week 4 step 4, the columns that decide the result (bias, sigma_p/p, prim/Bethe) are off-screen.

## Content problems I checked against the files

- **week10 · 交互玩具模型:** the toy says "B = 10 mT ⊙ 指出纸面 (+x)". With z → and y ↑, +x points *into* the page, which is what the 10.1 figure says ("垂直纸面向里"). The toy label is wrong.
- **week07:** a heading "## 8.2 从理想磁场到实测场图" sits inside Week 7, right after 7.1. The English version has the same number.
- **week10 · 常见错误:** the heading says 四种, but the text says "它是五个错误中代价最大的". There are three boxes plus W10-a, which makes four.
- **week04 · RunAction.cc:** a comment says "~28 MeV at 100 MeV kinetic energy". The page says about 24 MeV, and the expected output is 23.95 MeV.
- **Term inconsistency:** "event" is 事件 in Weeks 2–3 (the Week 2 title is 运行、事件、径迹、步) but 事例 in the glossary and every other week.
- **English in 中文 mode:** the "copy" buttons, the toy table labels "Entries N" and "transmission", and the Week 12 proposal template headings.
- **Motion:** with reduced motion on, the share GIFs still autoplay. (The 3D displays correctly start paused.)

- G1 readability = 3; G2 code/tables/figures on my screen = 2; G3 dark mode = 4; G5 3D/toys = 4
- F1 Chinese naturalness = 4; F3 term consistency = 3
- **NPS (J1) = 7**

The teaching design is excellent, and on a good network on the iPad it's a pleasure. But on my phone in China I lose the 3D views and half of every figure, and I couldn't run any Geant4 lab.

**The one change I want most:** self-host the fonts and three.js, and give students without admin rights a way to install Geant4 (conda-forge or a container, with China mirrors). Next after that: make the figures and canvas labels readable at phone width.
