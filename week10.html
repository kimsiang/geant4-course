Background · Spin and precession自旋与进动

Besides charge and mass, a muon has spin, which makes it a tiny magnet. In a magnetic field its spin axis turns around the field direction, like a tilted spinning top, at a frequency proportional to the field: 135.5 MHz per tesla. Measure the frequency and you have measured the field inside the material.

除了电荷和质量，缪子还有自旋，这让它成为一个微小的磁体。在磁场中，它的自旋轴绕磁场方向转动，就像一个倾斜旋转的陀螺，频率正比于磁场：每特斯拉 135.5 MHz。测出频率，就测出了材料内部的磁场。

Background · How the spin is seen怎样看到自旋

When a positive muon decays, its positron is more likely to fly out along the spin direction. Two counters on opposite sides therefore see their rates rise and fall in turn as the spin rotates. Their normalised difference, the asymmetry, oscillates at the precession frequency.

正缪子衰变时，正电子更倾向于沿自旋方向飞出。所以随着自旋转动，两侧相对的计数器计数率此起彼伏。两者归一化后的差值，即不对称度，以进动频率振荡。

For muography projects用于缪子成像工程

μSR is not muography: it uses accelerator muons stopped in small samples to study magnetism and superconductivity. On the muography track this week is optional. Worth keeping: field maps must be printed and checked before use, and a fit that converges can still be meaningless (the no-spin control).

μSR 不是缪子成像：它把加速器缪子停在小样品中，研究磁性和超导。对缪子成像方向来说，本周为选修。值得记住的有两点：场图在使用前必须打印出来核对；拟合收敛不代表结果有意义（不加自旋的对照运行就是例子）。

Theorem 10.1 · Muon spin precession缪子自旋进动

A stopped μ⁺ in a transverse field B precesses at f = (γμ/2π)·B, where γμ is the muon gyromagnetic ratio and γμ/2π = 135.5388 MHz/T. At 10 mT: f = 1.3554 MHz, period 0.738 µs, about three turns per lifetime (τμ = 2.1970 µs). Direction: dS/dt = γμ S × B with γμ > 0, so a spin starting along −z in B ∥ +x turns towards −y first.

横向磁场 B 中静止的 μ⁺ 以 f = (γμ/2π)·B 进动，其中 γμ 是缪子的旋磁比，γμ/2π = 135.5388 MHz/T。10 mT 时 f = 1.3554 MHz，周期 0.738 µs，每个寿命（τμ = 2.1970 µs）内约转三圈。方向：dS/dt = γμ S × B，γμ > 0，所以在 B ∥ +x 中初始沿 −z 的自旋先转向 −y。

Definition 10.1 · Decay asymmetry衰变不对称度

The positron from μ⁺ → e⁺ ν ν̄ is emitted preferentially along the muon spin: dN/dΩ ∝ 1 + a cos θ. The asymmetry depends on the positron energy: with ε = E/E_max (E_max = 52.83 MeV), a(ε) = (2ε − 1)/(3 − 2ε). It is negative below ε = 1/2, zero at ε = 1/2 and reaches 1 at the end point; averaged over the whole spectrum it is a = 1/3. An energy threshold or an absorber in front of a counter therefore raises the effective asymmetry, at the cost of rate. With two opposite counters, N_U,D(t) ∝ e^(−t/τ)(1 ∓ A0 sin ωt) and A(t) = (N_D − N_U)/(N_D + N_U) = A0 sin(ωt + φ): the lifetime envelope cancels and the precession remains.

μ⁺ → e⁺ ν ν̄ 的正电子倾向于沿缪子自旋方向发射：dN/dΩ ∝ 1 + a cos θ。不对称度依赖于正电子能量：令 ε = E/E_max（E_max = 52.83 MeV），则 a(ε) = (2ε − 1)/(3 − 2ε)。ε < 1/2 时为负，ε = 1/2 时为零，在端点处达到 1；对整个能谱平均为 a = 1/3。因此在计数器前加能量阈或吸收体，会以计数率为代价提高有效不对称度。用两个相对的计数器，N_U,D(t) ∝ e^(−t/τ)(1 ∓ A0 sin ωt)，A(t) = (N_D − N_U)/(N_D + N_U) = A0 sin(ωt + φ)：寿命包络相消，进动留了下来。

What Geant4 simulates here, and what it does not这里 Geant4 模拟了什么，没模拟什么

Geant4 and musrSim simulate the instrument: the beam, the field, where the muon stops, the decay and the positron detection. They do not simulate the sample's spin physics: no local or internal fields, no nuclear dipoles (Kubo–Toyabe relaxation), no muonium. You put the sample in by hand, as the precession frequency and a relaxation rate you impose. So the simulation answers instrument questions (rates, asymmetry, backgrounds, field homogeneity), not what a material does to the muon.

Geant4 和 musrSim 模拟的是仪器：束流、磁场、缪子停在哪里、衰变以及正电子探测。它们不模拟样品中的自旋物理：没有局域场或内场，没有核偶极（Kubo–Toyabe 弛豫），也没有缪子素。样品要你手工放进去，以你设定的进动频率和弛豫率出现。所以模拟回答的是仪器问题（计数率、不对称度、本底、磁场均匀度），而不是材料对缪子做了什么。

10.3 · Optical photons are a fidelity switch10.3 · 光学光子是一个保真度开关

/musr/command G4OpticalPhotons true    # off by default: photon counts explode

Optical transport means something only when the materials carry optical properties. It adds branches such as nOptPhot and odet_nPhot. Ask which observable needs information a parameterised response cannot keep: photon arrival times, surfaces, photocathode response. The muSR asymmetry needs only which counter fired and when, so it stays off this week (Week 5's parameterisation versus optical transport, again).

只有材料带有光学属性时，光学输运才有意义。它会增加 nOptPhot、odet_nPhot 等分支。要问：哪个可观测量需要参数化响应保留不了的信息？光子到达时间、表面、光阴极响应。μSR 不对称度只需要哪个计数器何时响应，所以本周不打开它（又一次是第 5 周的参数化与光学输运之争）。

TOY MODEL · approximations近似

        
Every muon stops at the target centre at t = 0 (in Geant4 it takes a few ns). Positron direction from 1 + a·S·n with a = 1/3, the energy-averaged Michel value, with no energy threshold and no copper or plastic in the way. Counters are cones of half-angle α around ±y; 44° matches the 80 mm wide slabs 42 mm from the target. The toy therefore expects A0 = a·P·(1 + cos α)/2 = 0.29. Our Geant4 run gives A0 = 0.241 ± 0.009. Energy loss alone would not lower A0 (absorbing the slow positrons, whose a(ε) is small or negative, raises it). The drop comes from multiple scattering in up to 15 mm of copper (about one radiation length) for positrons emitted along ±y, which smears their directions, and from the square-slab acceptance, which differs from a cone. Field spread is Gaussian per muon. The frequency, its error and the fitted B behave as in the real analysis.

        
每个缪子都在 t = 0 时停在靶中心（在 Geant4 中需要几 ns）。正电子方向按 1 + a·S·n 抽样，a = 1/3 为能谱平均的 Michel 值，没有能量阈，也没有铜或塑料挡路。计数器被近似为绕 ±y 轴、半张角为 α 的圆锥；44° 对应距靶 42 mm、宽 80 mm 的板。所以玩具模型预期 A0 = a·P·(1 + cos α)/2 = 0.29。我们的 Geant4 运行给出 A0 = 0.241 ± 0.009。单靠能量损失并不会降低 A0（吸收掉 a(ε) 很小甚至为负的慢正电子反而会提高它）。下降来自沿 ±y 发射的正电子在最多 15 mm 铜（约一个辐射长度）中的多次散射，它抹平了方向；以及方形板与圆锥不同的接受度。磁场弥散按每个缪子的高斯分布处理。频率、其误差以及拟合出的 B 与真实分析的行为相同。

// Usage:  ./week10 tf.mac            spin-aware muon decay (the physics a muSR study needs)
//         ./week10 tf.mac nospin     stock decay only: what an application gets if nobody adds spin
#include "ActionInitialization.hh"
#include "DetectorConstruction.hh"

#include "FTFP_BERT.hh"
#include "G4RunManagerFactory.hh"
#include "G4SpinDecayPhysics.hh"
#include "G4UIExecutive.hh"
#include "G4UImanager.hh"
#include "G4VisExecutive.hh"

#include <cstring>

int main(int argc, char** argv)
{
  auto* runManager =
    G4RunManagerFactory::CreateRunManager(G4RunManagerType::Default);
  runManager->SetNumberOfThreads(2);

  runManager->SetUserInitialization(new DetectorConstruction());

  auto* physics = new FTFP_BERT();
  const bool withSpin = !(argc > 2 && std::strcmp(argv[2], "nospin") == 0);
  if (withSpin) {
    // THE line of this week. FTFP_BERT's G4DecayPhysics uses G4Decay + G4MuonDecayChannel,
    // which "neglects muon polarization": no precession at rest, isotropic positrons, no asymmetry.
    // G4SpinDecayPhysics swaps in G4DecayWithSpin and G4MuonDecayChannelWithSpin.
    // Register it AFTER G4DecayPhysics (FTFP_BERT already contains it): it replaces "Decay".
    physics->RegisterPhysics(new G4SpinDecayPhysics());
  }
  runManager->SetUserInitialization(physics);
  runManager->SetUserInitialization(new ActionInitialization());
  // No Initialize() here: the macro sets /tf/B first, then calls /run/initialize.

  auto* uiManager = G4UImanager::GetUIpointer();
  if (argc > 1) {
    uiManager->ApplyCommand("/control/execute " + G4String(argv[1]));
  } else {
    auto* ui = new G4UIExecutive(argc, argv);
    auto* visManager = new G4VisExecutive();
    visManager->Initialize();
    uiManager->ApplyCommand("/control/execute vis.mac");
    ui->SessionStart();
    delete visManager;
    delete ui;
  }

  delete runManager;
  return 0;
}

G4bool CounterSD::ProcessHits(G4Step* step, G4TouchableHistory*)
{
  const G4double edep = step->GetTotalEnergyDeposit();
  if (edep <= 0.) return false;

  const G4int id = pre->GetTouchable()->GetCopyNumber();   // 11 = up, 1 = down
  const G4double t = pre->GetGlobalTime();
  fEdep[id] += edep;
  auto it = fTime.find(id);
  if (it == fTime.end() || t < it->second) fTime[id] = t;
  return true;
}

    """asymmetry.py -- recover the field from the precession of the muon spin (annotated).

Reads a t1 tree with musrSim branch names: the course app writes data/tf_10mT.root;
musrSim writes data/musrSim_0_tf.root for tf_musr.mac (run number 0, output name "tf").
Usage: python3 asymmetry.py [file.root] [--measured]
  --measured  use the counter's det_time_start instead of the truth muDecayTime
"""
import sys

import awkward as ak
import numpy as np
import uproot
from scipy.optimize import curve_fit

GAMMA_MU = 135.5388          # MHz/T, gamma_mu / 2pi (PDG / CODATA)

args = [a for a in sys.argv[1:] if not a.startswith("--")]
fname = args[0] if args else "data/musrSim_0_tf.root"
measured = "--measured" in sys.argv

D = uproot.open(fname)["t1"].arrays(
    ["det_ID", "det_time_start", "muDecayTime", "muDecayDetID"], library="ak")
sel = (D.muDecayDetID == 5) & (D.muDecayTime > 0)       # stopped AND decayed in the target; no sentinels
D = D[sel]
print(f"muons decayed in target: {len(D)}")

hitU = np.asarray(ak.any(D.det_ID == 11, axis=1))       # positron reached the UP counter (y = +45 mm)
hitD = np.asarray(ak.any(D.det_ID == 1, axis=1))        # ... the DOWN counter (y = -45 mm)
if measured:
    # what the electronics would see: the first time each counter fired
    tU = np.asarray(ak.fill_none(ak.firsts(D.det_time_start[D.det_ID == 11]), -1.0))[hitU]
    tD = np.asarray(ak.fill_none(ak.firsts(D.det_time_start[D.det_ID == 1]), -1.0))[hitD]
else:
    t = np.asarray(D.muDecayTime)                       # Monte Carlo truth (our choice: no detector timing)
    tU, tD = t[hitU], t[hitD]
print(f"positrons in U (ID 11): {len(tU)}   in D (ID 1): {len(tD)}")

bins = np.linspace(0, 10, 201)                          # 200 bins of 50 ns; T = 0.74 us at 10 mT
NU, _ = np.histogram(tU, bins=bins)
ND, _ = np.histogram(tD, bins=bins)
centre = 0.5 * (bins[:-1] + bins[1:])
tot = NU + ND
ok = tot > 20
A = (ND[ok] - NU[ok]) / tot[ok]                         # D first: mu+ spin turns from -z towards -y
Aerr = np.sqrt((1.0 - A**2) / tot[ok])                  # binomial split of tot counts
tg = centre[ok]


def osc(t, A0, f, phi, lam):
    return A0 * np.exp(-lam * t) * np.sin(2 * np.pi * f * t + phi)


# Starting values from a periodogram, not from the field you expect: at each trial
# frequency fit A = a sin(2 pi f t) + b cos(2 pi f t) by weighted least squares and keep
# the f with the smallest chi2. A start fixed at the 10 mT value (1.35 MHz) makes the
# fit lock onto a wrong minimum at other fields, and nothing would warn you.
w = 1.0 / Aerr**2
best = None
for ftry in np.arange(0.10, 8.0, 0.002):
    X = np.column_stack([np.sin(2 * np.pi * ftry * tg), np.cos(2 * np.pi * ftry * tg)])
    coef, *_ = np.linalg.lstsq(X * np.sqrt(w)[:, None], A * np.sqrt(w), rcond=None)
    c2 = np.sum(w * (A - X @ coef) ** 2)
    if best is None or c2 < best[0]:
        best = (c2, ftry, coef)
_, f0, (a0, b0) = best
popt, pcov = curve_fit(osc, tg, A, p0=[np.hypot(a0, b0), f0, np.arctan2(b0, a0), 0.0],
                       sigma=Aerr, absolute_sigma=True)
perr = np.sqrt(np.diag(pcov))
chi2 = np.sum(((A - osc(tg, *popt)) / Aerr) ** 2)
ndf = len(A) - 4                                        # bins with tot <= 20 are dropped, so ndf can change by one or two between runs
f, df = popt[1], perr[1]
print(f"A0  = {popt[0]:.4f} +/- {perr[0]:.4f}")
print(f"f   = {f:.4f} +/- {df:.4f} MHz")
print(f"phi = {popt[2]:+.3f} +/- {perr[2]:.3f} rad    lambda = {popt[3]:.4f} +/- {perr[3]:.4f} /us")
print(f"chi2/ndf = {chi2:.1f}/{ndf}")
if abs(popt[0]) < 3 * perr[0]:
    # A fit always returns numbers. With no oscillation, f is just the largest noise wiggle.
    print("WARNING: A0 is consistent with zero: there is no oscillation, so f and B are meaningless")
print(f"B   = {f / GAMMA_MU * 1000:.3f} +/- {df / GAMMA_MU * 1000:.3f} mT")

    """field_scan.py -- Lab 10: fitted precession frequency versus configured field.
Usage: python3 field_scan.py data/tf_5mT.root data/tf_10mT.root data/tf_20mT.root data/tf_30mT.root
The configured field is read from the file name (tf_<B>mT.root).
"""
import re
import subprocess
import sys

import numpy as np

GAMMA_MU = 135.5388   # MHz/T

B, F, dF = [], [], []
for fn in sys.argv[1:]:
    out = subprocess.run([sys.executable, "asymmetry.py", fn], capture_output=True, text=True, check=True).stdout
    f, df = map(float, re.search(r"f   = ([\d.]+) \+/- ([\d.]+)", out).groups())
    b = float(re.search(r"tf_(\d+)mT", fn).group(1)) / 1000.0
    if "WARNING" in out:
        # asymmetry.py found no significant oscillation: its f is noise, not a measurement
        print(f"B = {b*1000:5.1f} mT   NO OSCILLATION (A0 consistent with zero): excluded from the fit")
        continue
    B.append(b); F.append(f); dF.append(df)
    print(f"B = {b*1000:5.1f} mT   f = {f:.4f} +/- {df:.4f} MHz   expected {GAMMA_MU*b:.4f}   pull {(f-GAMMA_MU*b)/df:+.2f}")

if len(B) < 2:
    sys.exit("fewer than two fields with a significant oscillation: no slope")
B, F, dF = map(np.asarray, (B, F, dF))
w = 1 / dF**2
# weighted straight line through the origin: f = k B
k = np.sum(w * B * F) / np.sum(w * B * B)
dk = 1 / np.sqrt(np.sum(w * B * B))
chi2 = np.sum(w * (F - k * B) ** 2)
print(f"slope k = {k:.3f} +/- {dk:.3f} MHz/T   (gamma_mu/2pi = {GAMMA_MU})   chi2/ndf = {chi2:.2f}/{len(B)-1}")
