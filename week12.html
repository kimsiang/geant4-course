A research question for a muography project fits in one sentence.一个缪子成像项目的研究问题，一句话就能说清。

Background · Example questions问题示例

Can a 1 m² telescope in the tunnel at chainage 2+300 detect a 3 m void within 20 m above the crown in one month? How precisely can three months of data measure the mean density of the overburden above a planned shaft? Does a 5 cm lead absorber improve the contrast enough to pay for its weight?

隧道里程 2+300 处的一台 1 m² 望远镜，能否在一个月内探测到拱顶以上 20 m 范围内一个 3 m 的空洞？三个月的数据能把拟建竖井上方覆盖层的平均密度测到多准？5 cm 厚的铅吸收体带来的对比度提升，值不值得它的重量？

Background · Significance of a feature特征的显著性

If the model without the void predicts N muons along a direction and the void adds ΔN, the feature stands out by about ΔN/√N standard deviations. Both N and ΔN grow with exposure time, so significance grows only as √time. That is the 1/√N rule of Week 1 again, now deciding how long the instrument stays underground. The growth stops at the systematic floor: over long exposures the flux model (10–20% at large zenith angles) and the rock density dominate, so a ratio to an open-sky or reference direction usually buys more than extra months.

若无空洞的模型在某个方向上预测 N 个缪子，空洞使其增加 ΔN，则这个特征的显著性约为 ΔN/√N 个标准差。N 和 ΔN 都随测量时间增长，所以显著性只按 √时间 增长。这又是第 1 周的 1/√N 规律，只是现在它决定仪器要在地下待多久。这种增长止于系统误差的下限：长时间曝光时，通量模型（大天顶角处 10–20%）和岩石密度占主导，所以与开阔天空或参考方向取比值，通常比多测几个月更有用。

For muography projects用于缪子成像工程

Use the feasibility planner on this page with your site's numbers. A good muography proposal states the target (void size, density contrast), the overburden and its uncertainty, the detector area and acceptance, the exposure time, and the significance it expects, before any data are taken.

用本页的可行性规划器代入你场地的数字。一份好的缪子成像提案应当在采集任何数据之前写明：目标（空洞尺寸、密度对比）、覆盖层厚度及其不确定度、探测器面积和接受度、测量时间，以及预期的显著性。

Muography exposure estimate缪子成像曝光估算

      
For one line-of-sight bin: N = I(θ) · A · ΔΩ · T · transmission, with I(θ) = Iv cos²θ. The void's signal is a fractional excess ΔN/N. A systematic that does not cancel (flux model, rock density) caps the significance. An estimate for a proposal, not a simulation: take the transmission from your own Geant4 run or the range table.

      
对一个视线方向的 bin：N = I(θ) · A · ΔΩ · T · 透射率，I(θ) = Iv cos²θ。空洞的信号是一个相对超出 ΔN/N。不能相消的系统误差（通量模型、岩石密度）会封顶显著性。这是写提案用的估算，不是模拟：透射率请取自你自己的 Geant4 运行或射程表。

TOY MODEL · approximations近似

        
Statistical precision: σ/ε = √((1 − ε)/(εN)) for an efficiency, r/√(εN) for a mean. The total is √(stat² + sys²), so it never falls below the systematic. Cost: constant CPU per generated event, with a default of 0.34 ms from our Week 10 run (200 000 muons, about 68 CPU-seconds on two threads). It assumes perfect scaling over cores and no queue, failed jobs or analysis time. "Feasible" means production fits in half the days left. Measure your own ms per event with a pilot run; it changes by orders of magnitude with geometry, energy and optical photons.

        
统计精度：效率为 σ/ε = √((1 − ε)/(εN))，均值为 r/√(εN)。总不确定度为 √(stat² + sys²)，所以永远不会低于系统误差。代价：每个产生事例的 CPU 时间恒定，默认 0.34 ms，取自我们第 10 周的运行（20 万个缪子，双线程约 68 CPU 秒）。假设多核完美扩展，不考虑排队、失败作业和分析时间。“可行”指生产只占剩余天数的一半。请用试跑测出你自己的每事例耗时；它会随几何、能量和光学光子相差几个数量级。

Definition 12.2 · Defence weights答辩权重

        
A defence is graded on six parts: the problem 20%, the implementation 20%, validation 25%, analysis 15%, communication 10%, and code and reproducibility 10%. Validation carries the most weight on its own.

        
答辩按六个部分评分：问题 20%，实现 20%，验证 25%，分析 15%，表达 10%，代码与可复现性 10%。单项中验证的权重最大。

      

      

        
Example 12.1 · A ten-minute defence十分钟答辩

        
1 min: the physics question, clear without slides. 3 min: simulation design and the key implementation choices. 3 min: validation, with uncertainties and the reference. 1 min: known limitations. The rest: questions, at least one of them a referee's question.

        
1 分钟：物理问题，不看幻灯片也能听懂。3 分钟：模拟设计和关键实现选择。3 分钟：验证，含不确定度和参照。1 分钟：已知局限。其余时间：提问，其中至少一个是审稿人式的问题。

      

      

        
Required boundary statement必需的边界声明

        
Name at least one physically important effect your virtual experiment ignores, and its consequence. If you used musrSim, also name one application-specific behaviour (a magic volume name, a fork-dependent command, a parameterised response, the physics list) and how you verified it against the installed source. Course-W10-a is a model answer.

        
至少说出一个被你的虚拟实验忽略的重要物理效应及其后果。如果用了 musrSim，还要说出一个应用特有的行为（特殊体积名、依赖分支的命令、参数化响应、物理列表），以及你如何对照已安装的源码核实它。Course-W10-a 就是一个范例答案。

      

      
The 10% for code and reproducibility is checked by doing it: an evaluator clones the repository, rebuilds from scratch, runs the macro and gets the headline number without asking you anything. Record the executable version, the Geant4/ROOT environment, macros, seed strategy, thread count and the analysis script.

      
代码与可复现性这 10% 是靠实际操作来检查的：评审克隆仓库、从零编译、运行宏，不问你任何问题就得到核心数字。要记录可执行程序版本、Geant4/ROOT 环境、宏文件、种子策略、线程数和分析脚本。

    #ifndef SkyFlux_h
#define SkyFlux_h 1

// Sea-level cosmic-muon intensity: the Gaisser parametrisation with the
// Guan et al. (2015) correction for low energies and large zenith angles
// (M. Guan et al., arXiv:1509.06176, Eq. 3; the cos(theta*) curvature
// correction of D. Chirkin, hep-ph/0407078).
//
//   dI/dE dOmega = 0.14 [E (1 + 3.64 GeV / (E cos^1.29 th*))]^-2.7
//                  x [ 1/(1 + 1.1 E cos th*/115 GeV) + 0.054/(1 + 1.1 E cos th*/850 GeV) ]
//                  in cm^-2 s^-1 sr^-1 GeV^-1, E in GeV
//
// Approximations (state them in any result you quote):
//  - one intensity for mu+ and mu-; the charge is drawn with mu+/mu- = 1.27
//    (a typical sea-level value; it depends on energy and site)
//  - sea level, no altitude, geomagnetic or seasonal correction
//  - above ~100 TeV, prompt muons and the knee are ignored (irrelevant here)
//  - the parametrisation is good to roughly 10-20% in absolute flux: this is
//    the "flux-model systematic" of the analysis.

#include <cmath>

namespace SkyFlux
{
// cos(theta*) of the curved atmosphere (Chirkin), from the geometric cos(theta)
inline double CosThetaStar(double c)
{
  const double P1 = 0.102573, P2 = -0.068287, P3 = 0.958633, P4 = 0.0407253, P5 = 0.817285;
  const double num = c * c + P1 * P1 + P2 * std::pow(c, P3) + P4 * std::pow(c, P5);
  const double den = 1. + P1 * P1 + P2 + P4;
  return std::sqrt(num / den);
}

// differential intensity, cm^-2 s^-1 sr^-1 GeV^-1; E in GeV, c = cos(zenith)
inline double Intensity(double E, double c)
{
  const double cs = CosThetaStar(c);
  const double Eeff = E * (1. + 3.64 / (E * std::pow(cs, 1.29)));
  return 0.14 * std::pow(Eeff, -2.7) *
         (1. / (1. + 1.1 * E * cs / 115.) + 0.054 / (1. + 1.1 * E * cs / 850.));
}

// Upper bound used by the rejection sampler: Eeff >= E and the bracket <= 1.054,
// so Intensity(E, c) * c <= 0.14 * 1.054 * E^-2.7 for every c in [0, 1].
inline double Envelope(double E) { return 0.14 * 1.054 * std::pow(E, -2.7); }

// Flux through a HORIZONTAL plane, integrated over E in [emin, emax] GeV and
// cos(theta) in [cmin, 1], all azimuths: cm^-2 s^-1. Midpoint rule in log E and cos.
inline double PlaneFlux(double emin, double emax, double cmin)
{
  const int nE = 1200, nC = 200;
  const double lmin = std::log(emin), lmax = std::log(emax);
  double sum = 0.;
  for (int i = 0; i < nE; ++i) {
    const double E = std::exp(lmin + (i + 0.5) * (lmax - lmin) / nE);
    double inner = 0.;
    for (int j = 0; j < nC; ++j) {
      const double c = cmin + (j + 0.5) * (1. - cmin) / nC;
      inner += Intensity(E, c) * c;
    }
    sum += inner * (1. - cmin) / nC * E * (lmax - lmin) / nE;   // dE = E dlnE
  }
  return 2. * M_PI * sum;
}
}  // namespace SkyFlux

G4VPhysicalVolume* DetectorConstruction::Construct()
{
  auto* nist = G4NistManager::Instance();
  auto* air = nist->FindOrBuildMaterial("G4_AIR");
  auto* scint = nist->FindOrBuildMaterial("G4_PLASTIC_SC_VINYLTOLUENE");

  // PDG "standard rock": <Z> = 11, <A> = 22, rho = 2.65 g/cm3 (Z/A = 0.5).
  // A single pseudo-element is exactly how the PDG defines it for muon tables.
  auto* rock = G4Material::GetMaterial("StandardRock", false);
  if (!rock) rock = new G4Material("StandardRock", 11., 22. * g / mole, 2.65 * g / cm3);

  const G4double rockHalfZ = 0.5 * (fOverburden + 2. * kTunnelHalf + kFloorRock);
  const G4double rockCentreZ = -rockHalfZ;   // top face at z = 0
  const G4double worldHalfXY = fRockHalfXY + 10. * m;
  const G4double worldHalfZ = 2. * rockHalfZ + 10. * m;

  auto* logicWorld = new G4LogicalVolume(new G4Box("World", worldHalfXY, worldHalfXY, worldHalfZ), air, "World");
  auto* physWorld = new G4PVPlacement(nullptr, {}, logicWorld, "World", nullptr, false, 0, true);

  auto* logicRock = new G4LogicalVolume(new G4Box("Rock", fRockHalfXY, fRockHalfXY, rockHalfZ), rock, "Rock");
  new G4PVPlacement(nullptr, G4ThreeVector(0, 0, rockCentreZ), logicRock, "Rock", logicWorld, false, 0, true);

  // tunnel: an air box inside the rock, running along y
  const G4double tunnelLocalZ = TunnelCentreZ() - rockCentreZ;
  auto* logicTunnel = new G4LogicalVolume(new G4Box("Tunnel", kTunnelHalf, fRockHalfXY, kTunnelHalf), air, "Tunnel");
  new G4PVPlacement(nullptr, G4ThreeVector(0, 0, tunnelLocalZ), logicTunnel, "Tunnel", logicRock, false, 0, true);

  // two 1 cm scintillator planes, copy 0 = top, 1 = bottom (positions in tunnel coordinates)
  auto* logicPlane = new G4LogicalVolume(new G4Box("Plane", fPlaneHalf, fPlaneHalf, 0.5 * cm), scint, "Plane");
  new G4PVPlacement(nullptr, G4ThreeVector(0, 0, +0.5 * fPlaneSep), logicPlane, "Plane", logicTunnel, false, 0, true);
  new G4PVPlacement(nullptr, G4ThreeVector(0, 0, -0.5 * fPlaneSep), logicPlane, "Plane", logicTunnel, false, 1, true);

  // the target: an air-filled void in the rock above the tunnel
  if (fVoidOn) {
    if (fVoidZ + fVoidR > 0. || fVoidZ - fVoidR < -fOverburden)
      G4Exception("DetectorConstruction", "MUO001", FatalException,
                  "The void must lie inside the rock between the ground and the tunnel ceiling.");
    auto* logicVoid = new G4LogicalVolume(new G4Orb("Void", fVoidR), air, "Void");
    new G4PVPlacement(nullptr, G4ThreeVector(fVoidX, fVoidY, fVoidZ - rockCentreZ), logicVoid, "Void", logicRock,
                      false, 0, true);
  }

  G4cout << "Muography starter: overburden " << fOverburden / m << " m, rock to z = " << -2. * rockHalfZ / m
         << " m, planes " << 2. * fPlaneHalf / m << " m x " << 2. * fPlaneHalf / m << " m, " << fPlaneSep / m
         << " m apart, top plane at z = " << TopPlaneZ() / m << " m, void "
         << (fVoidOn ? "ON" : "off") << G4endl;
  return physWorld;
}

class G4ParticleGun;

// Cosmic muons from the sky, aimed at the telescope ("importance region").
// For each event:
//   1. draw (E, cos theta) with density  I(E, theta) * cos theta  (flux through a
//      horizontal plane, Guan et al. 2015), and phi uniform;
//   2. draw an AIM POINT uniformly on a horizontal square around the top plane
//      (plane + margin);
//   3. start the muon on the straight line through the aim point, 0.5 m above
//      the ground, travelling downwards.
// Because the aim points are uniform and the directions follow the plane flux,
// every event represents the same exposure time:
//   dt = 1 / (Phi * A_aim),  Phi = flux through a horizontal plane in the sampled
//   E and theta ranges [cm^-2 s^-1], A_aim = aim-square area [cm^2].
// Muons that would reach the telescope from OUTSIDE the aim square after
// scattering in the rock are missed: keep the margin larger than the lateral
// spread (about 0.2 m for 20 GeV muons through 30 m of rock).
class PrimaryGeneratorAction : public G4VUserPrimaryGeneratorAction
{
  public:
    PrimaryGeneratorAction();
    ~PrimaryGeneratorAction() override;
    void GeneratePrimaries(G4Event* event) override;

  private:
    G4ParticleGun* fGun = nullptr;
    const DetectorConstruction* fDet = nullptr;
};

#endif

void PrimaryGeneratorAction::GeneratePrimaries(G4Event* event)
{
  const G4double emin = fDet->EMin() / GeV, emax = fDet->EMax() / GeV;
  const G4double cmin = std::cos(fDet->ThetaMax());
  const G4double a = 2.7, p = 1. - a;
  const G4double lo = std::pow(emin, p), hi = std::pow(emax, p);

  // 1. (E, cos theta) by rejection: proposal E^-2.7 x uniform cos theta,
  //    accepted with probability I(E,c) c / envelope(E) <= 1
  G4double E = 0., c = 1.;
  do {
    E = std::pow(lo + G4UniformRand() * (hi - lo), 1. / p);
    c = cmin + (1. - cmin) * G4UniformRand();
  } while (G4UniformRand() * SkyFlux::Envelope(E) > SkyFlux::Intensity(E, c) * c);
  const G4double s = std::sqrt(1. - c * c), phi = twopi * G4UniformRand();
  const G4ThreeVector dir(s * std::cos(phi), s * std::sin(phi), -c);   // downwards

  // 2. aim point on the square around the top plane
  const G4double h = fDet->AimHalf();
  const G4ThreeVector aim((2. * G4UniformRand() - 1.) * h, (2. * G4UniformRand() - 1.) * h, fDet->TopPlaneZ());

  // 3. back along the line to 0.5 m above the ground
  const G4double zStart = 0.5 * m;
  const G4ThreeVector start = aim - dir * ((zStart - aim.z()) / c);

  // charge: mu+/mu- = 1.27 (approximate sea-level ratio)
  const char* name = (G4UniformRand() < 1.27 / 2.27) ? "mu+" : "mu-";
  fGun->SetParticleDefinition(G4ParticleTable::GetParticleTable()->FindParticle(name));
  fGun->SetParticleEnergy(E * GeV - 105.658 * MeV);   // E is the total energy; the gun takes kinetic energy
  fGun->SetParticleMomentumDirection(dir);
  fGun->SetParticlePosition(start);
  fGun->GeneratePrimaryVertex(event);
}

  hce->AddHitsCollection(G4SDManager::GetSDMpointer()->GetCollectionID(fHits), fHits);

G4bool TelescopeSD::ProcessHits(G4Step* step, G4TouchableHistory*)
{
  if (step->GetTrack()->GetTrackID() != 1) return false;   // primary muon only (ideal tracker)
  const auto* pre = step->GetPreStepPoint();
  const G4int copy = pre->GetTouchable()->GetCopyNumber();   // 0 = top, 1 = bottom
  (*fHits)[copy]->Set(pre->GetPosition(), pre->GetGlobalTime(), pre->GetKineticEnergy());
  return true;
}

void EventAction::EndOfEventAction(const G4Event* event)
{
  if (fHitsID < 0) fHitsID = G4SDManager::GetSDMpointer()->GetCollectionID("PlaneHits");
  auto* hce = event->GetHCofThisEvent();
  if (!hce) return;
  auto* hits = static_cast<TelescopeHitsCollection*>(hce->GetHC(fHitsID));
  if (!hits || hits->entries() < 2) return;

  const TelescopeHit* top = (*hits)[0];
  const TelescopeHit* bot = (*hits)[1];
  const G4bool both = top->IsHit() && bot->IsHit();
  fRunAction->Count(top->IsHit(), both);
  if (!both) return;

  const G4PrimaryParticle* mu = event->GetPrimaryVertex()->GetPrimary();
  const G4ThreeVector d = mu->GetMomentumDirection();   // downward
  const G4ThreeVector p0 = top->GetPos(), p1 = bot->GetPos();
  const G4double dz = p0.z() - p1.z();                  // > 0

  auto* a = G4AnalysisManager::Instance();
  a->FillNtupleDColumn(0, 0, (mu->GetKineticEnergy() + mu->GetMass()) / GeV);
  a->FillNtupleDColumn(0, 1, -d.z());
  a->FillNtupleDColumn(0, 2, std::atan2(d.y(), d.x()));
  a->FillNtupleIColumn(0, 3, static_cast<G4int>(mu->GetCharge()));
  a->FillNtupleDColumn(0, 4, top->GetEkin() / GeV);
  a->FillNtupleDColumn(0, 5, p0.x() / m);
  a->FillNtupleDColumn(0, 6, p0.y() / m);
  a->FillNtupleDColumn(0, 7, p1.x() / m);
  a->FillNtupleDColumn(0, 8, p1.y() / m);
  a->FillNtupleDColumn(0, 9, (p0.x() - p1.x()) / dz);
  a->FillNtupleDColumn(0, 10, (p0.y() - p1.y()) / dz);
  a->FillNtupleDColumn(0, 11, d.x() / d.z());           // upward slope = (-d.x)/(-d.z)
  a->FillNtupleDColumn(0, 12, d.y() / d.z());
  a->AddNtupleRow(0);
}

RunAction::RunAction()
{

  G4AccumulableManager::Instance()->Register(fBoth);

  auto* analysis = G4AnalysisManager::Instance();
  analysis->SetDefaultFileType("root");
  analysis->SetFileName("muography");
  analysis->SetNtupleMerging(true);

  // One row per muon that crossed BOTH planes (what a two-plane telescope records).
  analysis->CreateNtuple("tel", "Muons through both tracker planes");
  analysis->CreateNtupleDColumn("E0_GeV");       // total energy at the ground (generated)
  analysis->CreateNtupleDColumn("cos_theta");    // generated zenith angle
  analysis->CreateNtupleDColumn("phi");          // generated azimuth (rad)
  analysis->CreateNtupleIColumn("charge");
  analysis->CreateNtupleDColumn("Etel_GeV");     // kinetic energy at the top plane
  analysis->CreateNtupleDColumn("x0_m");         // top plane hit
  analysis->CreateNtupleDColumn("y0_m");
  analysis->CreateNtupleDColumn("x1_m");         // bottom plane hit
  analysis->CreateNtupleDColumn("y1_m");
  analysis->CreateNtupleDColumn("sx_rec");       // reconstructed slopes of the upward direction:
  analysis->CreateNtupleDColumn("sy_rec");       //   sx = dx/dz, sy = dy/dz (tan of projected angles)
  analysis->CreateNtupleDColumn("sx_true");      // the same from the generated direction
  analysis->CreateNtupleDColumn("sy_true");
  analysis->FinishNtuple();
}

void RunAction::EndOfRunAction(const G4Run* run)
{
  G4AccumulableManager::Instance()->Merge();
  auto* analysis = G4AnalysisManager::Instance();

  if (IsMaster() && run->GetNumberOfEvent() > 0) {
    const auto* det =
      static_cast<const DetectorConstruction*>(G4RunManager::GetRunManager()->GetUserDetectorConstruction());
    const G4int n = run->GetNumberOfEvent();
    const G4double emin = det->EMin() / GeV, emax = det->EMax() / GeV, cmin = std::cos(det->ThetaMax());
    const G4double thetaDeg = std::round(det->ThetaMax() / deg * 1000.) / 1000.;   // 60, not 60.0001
    const G4double phi = SkyFlux::PlaneFlux(emin, emax, cmin);                  // cm^-2 s^-1
    const G4double aim = std::pow(2. * det->AimHalf() / cm, 2);                 // cm^2
    const G4double dt = 1. / (phi * aim);                                       // s per generated muon
    const G4double live = n * dt;
    const G4int both = fBoth.GetValue();

    G4cout << "Run " << run->GetRunID() << ": " << n << " sky muons, E " << emin << "-" << emax
           << " GeV, theta < " << thetaDeg << " deg" << G4endl
           << "  flux through a horizontal plane in that range = " << phi << " cm^-2 s^-1" << G4endl
           << "  aim square " << 2. * det->AimHalf() / m << " m x " << 2. * det->AimHalf() / m
           << " m  ->  exposure " << live << " s (" << live / 3600. << " h)" << G4endl
           << "  top plane hit " << fTop.GetValue() << ", both planes " << both << "  ->  "
           << both / live * 86400. << " +/- " << std::sqrt(static_cast<G4double>(both)) / live * 86400.
           << " telescope muons per day" << G4endl;

    G4String base = analysis->GetFileName();
    if (base.size() > 5 && base.substr(base.size() - 5) == ".root") base = base.substr(0, base.size() - 5);
    std::ofstream js(base + "_run.json");
    js << "{\n  \"n_generated\": " << n << ",\n  \"n_top\": " << fTop.GetValue() << ",\n  \"n_both\": " << both
       << ",\n  \"seconds_per_event\": " << dt << ",\n  \"plane_flux_cm2_s\": " << phi
       << ",\n  \"aim_area_cm2\": " << aim << ",\n  \"emin_GeV\": " << emin << ",\n  \"emax_GeV\": " << emax
       << ",\n  \"theta_max_deg\": " << thetaDeg << ",\n  \"overburden_m\": " << det->Overburden() / m
       << ",\n  \"plane_side_m\": " << 2. * det->PlaneHalf() / m << ",\n  \"plane_sep_m\": " << det->PlaneSep() / m
       << ",\n  \"top_plane_z_m\": " << det->TopPlaneZ() / m << ",\n  \"void_on\": " << (det->VoidOn() ? "true" : "false")
       << ",\n  \"void_xyz_m\": [" << det->VoidX() / m << ", " << det->VoidY() / m << ", " << det->VoidZ() / m
       << "],\n  \"void_radius_m\": " << det->VoidRadius() / m << "\n}\n";
  }
  analysis->Write();
  analysis->CloseFile();
}

    analyse.pyANALYSIS
    """Muography starter analysis: rates, the void signal, and the exposure you need.

    python3 analyse.py void_off void_on [--fabs 0.10] [--frel 0.02]

Reads <name>.root (ntuple "tel") and <name>_run.json for both runs.
Every simulated muon stands for the same exposure (seconds_per_event in the JSON),
so counts / (n_generated x seconds_per_event) is a rate a real telescope would see.

Three numbers matter:
  1. the rate in the void direction without and with the void (per day);
  2. the significance after T days of data taking, Z(T);
  3. the exposure T needed for 3 sigma and 5 sigma.
Two ways to predict the "no void" rate, with different systematics:
  - absolute: take the flux model at face value. Its normalisation is uncertain by
    10-20% (fabs), and that error does not shrink with more data;
  - relative: measure a control region at the same zenith angle (flat ground: same
    rock path) and scale it with the simulated window/control ratio. The flux
    normalisation cancels; what is left (frel, default 2%) is the model's angular
    shape plus detector non-uniformity.
"""
import argparse
import json

import matplotlib
matplotlib.use("Agg")
import matplotlib.pyplot as plt
import numpy as np
import uproot


def load(name):
    with open(name + "_run.json") as f:
        meta = json.load(f)
    t = uproot.open(name + ".root")["tel"].arrays(["sx_rec", "sy_rec"], library="np")
    live_days = meta["n_generated"] * meta["seconds_per_event"] / 86400.0
    return meta, t["sx_rec"], t["sy_rec"], live_days


def directions(sx, sy):
    u = np.stack([sx, sy, np.ones_like(sx)], axis=1)
    return u / np.linalg.norm(u, axis=1)[:, None]


def exposure(delta, var_rate, sys_rate, z):
    """Days T with delta*T = z*sqrt(var_rate*T + (sys_rate*T)^2); inf if systematics win."""
    den = delta**2 - (z * sys_rate) ** 2
    return z**2 * var_rate / den if den > 0 else np.inf


def main():
    ap = argparse.ArgumentParser()
    ap.add_argument("off")
    ap.add_argument("on")
    ap.add_argument("--fabs", type=float, default=0.10, help="flux-model normalisation systematic")
    ap.add_argument("--frel", type=float, default=0.02, help="residual systematic of the relative method")
    args = ap.parse_args()

    m_off, sx0, sy0, d_off = load(args.off)
    m_on, sx1, sy1, d_on = load(args.on)
    print(f"MC exposure: {d_off * 24:.2f} h (void off), {d_on * 24:.2f} h (void on)")
    print(f"telescope rate: {len(sx0) / d_off:,.0f} /day (void off), {len(sx1) / d_on:,.0f} /day (void on)")

    # the void as seen from the centre of the top plane
    v = np.array(m_on["void_xyz_m"]) - np.array([0.0, 0.0, m_on["top_plane_z_m"]])
    dist = np.linalg.norm(v)
    v /= dist
    r_ang = np.arcsin(min(1.0, m_on["void_radius_m"] / dist))
    zen_v = np.arccos(v[2])
    print(f"void direction: zenith {np.degrees(zen_v):.1f} deg, slopes ({v[0] / v[2]:.3f}, {v[1] / v[2]:.3f}), "
          f"angular radius {np.degrees(r_ang):.1f} deg")

    def regions(sx, sy):
        u = directions(sx, sy)
        ang = np.arccos(np.clip(u @ v, -1, 1))
        zen = np.arccos(u[:, 2])
        window = ang < r_ang
        control = (np.abs(zen - zen_v) < r_ang) & (ang > 2 * r_ang)
        return window.sum(), control.sum()

    W0, C0 = regions(sx0, sy0)
    W1, C1 = regions(sx1, sy1)
    rW0, rC0, rW1, rC1 = W0 / d_off, C0 / d_off, W1 / d_on, C1 / d_on
    excess = rW1 / rW0 - 1
    err = np.sqrt(1 / W1 + 1 / W0) * (rW1 / rW0)
    print(f"\nvoid window: {W0} -> {W1} muons in the simulation = {rW0:,.0f} -> {rW1:,.0f} per day, "
          f"excess {100 * excess:+.1f} +/- {100 * err:.1f} % (MC statistics)")
    print(f"control ring (same zenith, away from the void): {C0} -> {C1} muons, "
          f"ratio on/off {C1 / C0 * d_off / d_on:.3f}")

    # 1. absolute: reference = flux model (void-off simulation), normalisation error fabs
    delta_a = rW1 - rW0
    # 2. relative: prediction = k * control(on), k from the void-off simulation
    k = W0 / C0
    pred = k * rC1
    delta_r = rW1 - pred
    print("\nexposure for a real telescope (days of data):")
    print(f"{'method':<34}{'3 sigma':>10}{'5 sigma':>10}")
    for label, delta, var, sysr in (
        (f"absolute, flux model +/-{100 * args.fabs:.0f}%", delta_a, rW0, args.fabs * rW0),
        (f"relative to control, +/-{100 * args.frel:.0f}%", delta_r, pred + k * k * rC1, args.frel * pred),
    ):
        cells = []
        for z in (3, 5):
            t = exposure(delta, var, sysr, z)
            cells.append("never" if np.isinf(t) else (f"{t * 24:.1f} h" if t < 1 else f"{t:.1f} d"))
        print(f"{label:<34}{cells[0]:>10}{cells[1]:>10}")

    # figure: rate map, ratio map, Z(T)
    bins = np.linspace(-1.6, 1.6, 17)
    h0, _, _ = np.histogram2d(sx0, sy0, bins=[bins, bins])
    h1, _, _ = np.histogram2d(sx1, sy1, bins=[bins, bins])
    ratio = np.where(h0 >= 20, (h1 / d_on) / np.maximum(h0 / d_off, 1e-9), np.nan)
    fig, ax = plt.subplots(1, 3, figsize=(15, 4.6))
    im = ax[0].imshow((h0 / d_off).T, origin="lower", extent=[-1.6, 1.6, -1.6, 1.6], cmap="viridis")
    fig.colorbar(im, ax=ax[0], label="muons per day per bin")
    ax[0].set_title(f"void off: {m_off['overburden_m']:.0f} m of standard rock")
    im = ax[1].imshow(ratio.T, origin="lower", extent=[-1.6, 1.6, -1.6, 1.6], cmap="RdBu_r", vmin=0.6, vmax=1.4)
    fig.colorbar(im, ax=ax[1], label="rate ratio, void on / off")
    ax[1].add_patch(plt.Circle((v[0] / v[2], v[1] / v[2]), np.tan(r_ang) / v[2] ** 2, fill=False, ls="--", color="k"))
    ax[1].set_title("ratio map (dashed: void outline)")
    for a in ax[:2]:
        a.set_xlabel("slope dx/dz")
        a.set_ylabel("slope dy/dz")
    T = np.logspace(-2, 2, 200)
    ax[2].plot(T, delta_a * T / np.sqrt(rW0 * T + (args.fabs * rW0 * T) ** 2), label=f"absolute, {100 * args.fabs:.0f}% sys")
    ax[2].plot(T, delta_r * T / np.sqrt((pred + k * k * rC1) * T + (args.frel * pred * T) ** 2),
               label=f"relative, {100 * args.frel:.0f}% sys")
    for z in (3, 5):
        ax[2].axhline(z, color="grey", lw=0.8, ls=":")
    ax[2].set_xscale("log")
    ax[2].set_xlabel("exposure [days]")
    ax[2].set_ylabel("significance Z")
    ax[2].legend()
    ax[2].set_title("significance of the void")
    fig.tight_layout()
    fig.savefig("analyse.png", dpi=120)
    print("\nwrote analyse.png")


if __name__ == "__main__":
    main()

    Before you trust 311 000 muons per day, estimate it without Geant4. Approximate the energy loss as dE/dX = a + bE with a ≈ 2.2 MeV cm²/g and b ≈ 4.0 × 10⁻⁶ cm²/g for standard rock. A muon crosses a slant depth X if E > (a/b)(ebX − 1), which is 17.8 GeV vertically. Integrate the same sky intensity above that energy over the telescope acceptance: we get 287 000 per day.
    
          
    在相信每天 311 000 个缪子之前，先不用 Geant4 估算一下。把能损近似为 dE/dX = a + bE，对标准岩石 a ≈ 2.2 MeV cm²/g、b ≈ 4.0 × 10⁻⁶ cm²/g。缪子能穿过斜深 X 的条件是 E > (a/b)(ebX − 1)，竖直方向为 17.8 GeV。在望远镜接受度内对同一天空强度从该能量往上积分，得到每天 287 000 个。
    
          
    Geant4 gives 8% more. That is the expected direction: the a + bE formula treats every muon as losing the mean energy, while real losses fluctuate, and some muons below the mean threshold get through. Approximations in the starter起点项目中的近似

        
Sky: Gaisser formula with the Guan et al. (2015) correction, sea level, good to roughly 10–20% in absolute flux; μ⁺/μ⁻ = 1.27 for all energies; no altitude, geomagnetic or seasonal effects. Ground: flat. Rock: PDG standard rock (Z = 11, A = 22, 2.65 g/cm³), uniform. Generator: muons aimed at a 2 m × 2 m square around the 1 m × 1 m top plane; a muon scattered into the telescope from outside that square is missed (lateral spread is about 0.2 m for 20 GeV muons through 30 m of rock). Tracker: ideal planes that record only the primary muon, with no resolution and 100% efficiency. Physics: course test physics list, 1 m production cut.

        
天空：带 Guan 等（2015）修正的 Gaisser 公式，海平面，绝对通量精度约 10–20%；所有能量 μ⁺/μ⁻ = 1.27；不含海拔、地磁和季节效应。地面：平地。岩石：PDG 标准岩石（Z = 11，A = 22，2.65 g/cm³），均匀。产生器：缪子瞄准 1 m × 1 m 顶层周围 2 m × 2 m 的方形区域；从区域外被散射进望远镜的缪子会被漏掉（20 GeV 缪子穿过 30 m 岩石的横向偏移约 0.2 m）。径迹探测器：理想探测面，只记录初级缪子，无分辨率，效率 100%。物理：课程测试物理列表，产生阈 1 m。
