Definition 1.1 · Monte Carlo method蒙特卡罗方法

The Monte Carlo method is a class of numerical techniques that solve deterministic or stochastic problems through repeated random sampling随机抽样. You build a probabilistic model for the quantity sought, draw and tally samples repeatedly, estimate the true value from the sample statistic, and take the uncertainty from the statistical fluctuation.

蒙特卡罗方法是一类通过重复随机抽样random sampling来求解确定性或随机性问题的数值方法：为所求的量建立概率模型，反复抽样并计数，用样本统计量估计真值，并用统计涨落给出不确定度。

History历史

Stanisław Ulam and John von Neumann named the method after the Monaco casino in the 1940s, during nuclear-weapons research at Los Alamos; Ulam's uncle was a habitual gambler. Neutron transport through fissile material is nearly intractable analytically, but simulating many neutron histories by random sampling is feasible. Nearly every detector simulation in particle physics today, Geant4 included, descends from that work.

20 世纪 40 年代，乌拉姆（Stanisław Ulam）和冯·诺依曼在洛斯阿拉莫斯从事核武器研究时，用摩纳哥的赌城为这一方法命名——乌拉姆的叔叔是个嗜赌之人。中子在裂变材料中的输运几乎无法解析求解，但用随机抽样模拟大量中子历史却完全可行。今天粒子物理中几乎所有的探测器模拟，包括 Geant4，都源于这一脉。

Example 1.2 · Monte Carlo estimate of a telescope's transmission probability望远镜透射概率的蒙特卡罗估计

Both layers are squares of side L, separated by a distance d, with normals along the vertical. Sample N downward-going directions and N impact points on the upper layer, then count the number k of tracks that cross both layers. For this first pass use an isotropic downward hemisphere; a later step replaces it with the approximately cos²θ sea-level muon angular distribution. Keeping the beam model separate from the detector geometry is deliberate.

两层都是边长为 L 的正方形，间距为 d，法线沿竖直方向。抽样 N 个向下的方向和上层上的 N 个入射点，统计同时穿过两层的径迹数 k。第一版使用向下半球内的各向同性分布；后面一步换成近似 cos²θ 的海平面缪子角分布。把束流模型beam model与探测器几何分开，是有意为之。

The measured quantity is a conditional transmission probability: the fraction of trajectories that have already crossed the upper layer and then also cross the lower layer. It is not the full geometric acceptance of a detector exposed to a flux. The estimate and its uncertainty are

所测的量是条件透射概率：在已经穿过上层的径迹中，同时也穿过下层的比例。它不是探测器在通量照射下的完整几何接受度。估计值及其不确定度为

ε ≃ kN, σε ≃ ε(1 − ε)N (1.1)

The count k follows a binomial distribution二项分布 Bin(N, ε) with variance Nε(1 − ε); dividing by N² gives Eq. (1.1). Raising N by a factor of 100 shrinks σε by only a factor of 10. Every later Geant4 measurement in this course pays this statistical tax.

计数 k 服从二项分布binomial distribution Bin(N, ε)，方差为 Nε(1 − ε)；除以 N² 即得式 (1.1)。N 增加 100 倍，σε 只缩小 10 倍——这是本课程之后每一个 Geant4 测量都要缴纳的“统计税”。

Intuition直觉

A Monte Carlo simulation is an opinion poll. You cannot ask every voter, which here means enumerating every possible history. If you draw enough unbiased random samples, the sample fraction converges to the true fraction at a predictable rate, and that rate is the 1/√N law. From day one, this is the ruler for a simulation's credibility.

蒙特卡罗模拟就像民意调查：你不可能问遍每一位选民（即枚举每一种可能的物理历史），但只要抽取足够多无偏的随机样本，样本比例就会以可预期的速度收敛到真实比例，这个速度就是 1/√N 规律。从第一天起，它就是衡量模拟可信度的标尺。

TOY MODEL · approximations近似

Straight lines in vacuum: no energy loss, scattering or decay. Both layers are zero-thickness squares that detect every crossing. Impact points are uniform on the upper layer. Directions come from the selected beam model. The reference value integrates the same model, so agreement checks the sampling and the statistics, not the physics of real muons. The cos²θ shape is the PDG approximation for sea-level muons of a few GeV; it ignores the energy dependence of the angular distribution. Charges are drawn with μ⁺/μ⁻ ≈ 1.27 only for colour.

真空中的直线：没有能量损失、散射或衰变。两层都是零厚度的正方形，对每次穿越都 100% 响应。入射点在上层均匀分布，方向来自所选的束流模型。参考值对同一模型积分，因此两者一致只检验抽样和统计，并不检验真实缪子的物理。cos²θ 形状是 PDG 对几 GeV 海平面缪子的近似，忽略了角分布对能量的依赖。电荷按 μ⁺/μ⁻ ≈ 1.27 抽取，仅用于着色。

    telescope_transmission.pyANALYSIS
    import numpy as np


def sample_directions(N, rng):
    """Sample N directions uniformly in solid angle over the downward hemisphere.

    This is a toy directional model. It is not the cosine-weighted
    crossing distribution of an isotropic flux through a plane, and it is
    not a sea-level cosmic-ray muon spectrum.
    """
    # Isotropic direction sampling over a hemisphere: cos(theta) is uniform.
    cos_theta = rng.uniform(0.0, 1.0, N)
    theta = np.arccos(cos_theta)
    phi = rng.uniform(0.0, 2 * np.pi, N)
    return theta, phi


def transmission_probability(N, L, d, seed=0):
    """Monte Carlo estimate of a two-layer telescope's conditional transmission
    probability.

    L : side length of each square layer [m]
    d : vertical separation between the two layers [m]
    The upper-layer impact point is sampled uniformly in the square.
    """
    rng = np.random.default_rng(seed)
    theta, phi = sample_directions(N, rng)

    # Sample the impact point uniformly across the upper square.
    x0 = rng.uniform(-L / 2, L / 2, N)
    y0 = rng.uniform(-L / 2, L / 2, N)

    # Straight-line propagation to the lower layer.
    x1 = x0 + d * np.tan(theta) * np.cos(phi)
    y1 = y0 + d * np.tan(theta) * np.sin(phi)

    hit = (np.abs(x1) < L / 2) & (np.abs(y1) < L / 2)
    k = hit.sum()
    eps = k / N
    sigma = np.sqrt(eps * (1 - eps) / N)
    return eps, sigma


if __name__ == "__main__":
    for N in [1_000, 10_000, 100_000]:
        eps, sigma = transmission_probability(N, L=0.3, d=0.5)
        print(f"N={N:>7d} eps={eps:.4f} sigma={sigma:.4f} "
              f"rel.unc.={sigma/eps:.2%}")

  5. Give it a real sky: the cos²θ sea-level shape换上真实天空：海平面 cos²θ 分布

    Restore the original lines. At sea level the muon intensity per solid angle is approximately I(θ) ∝ cos²θ (PDG, Cosmic rays review, for muons of a few GeV).
